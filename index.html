About Me

I am an IT student currently exploring the basics of web development and user interface design. I am still a beginner when it comes to coding, but I am actively learning the foundations of HTML and CSS by building small projects like this portfolio.

Outside of programming, I really enjoy visual art, graphic design, and crafting clean, organized presentation decks. I love working with soft pastel aesthetics. My main goal right now is to step out of my comfort zone, improve my technical skills, and learn how to bring my design ideas to life.
